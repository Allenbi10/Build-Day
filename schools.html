01 — THE EDUCATIONAL VALUE

Learning through
digital creation.

Roblox Studio is a free development environment that enables students to design, build, test, and publish interactive experiences.

Build Day provides a clear objective, age-appropriate activities, and a defined timeline. Students learn through experimentation, feedback, and revision while developing habits that support design, technology, engineering, and communication.

02 — PROGRAM STRUCTURE

A focused week
of learning.

MONDAY

Receive the creative challenge

Students receive a shared theme that provides structure while allowing them to develop individual concepts.

TUESDAY

Learn Roblox Studio fundamentals

Beginner-friendly activities introduce the workspace, building tools, and essential concepts for creating a playable experience.

WEDNESDAY–THURSDAY

Build, test, and improve

Students develop a simple experience and use tips, tutorials, and examples to refine its design and functionality.

FRIDAY

Submit and present

Students submit their completed project and participate in an online showcase recognizing their work and progress.

04 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Program information.

Does a student need coding experience?

No. Build Day is designed for students who are new to game creation. Participants begin with introductory activities and progress at an appropriate pace.

What is the educational role of Roblox Studio?

Studio provides a visual environment for designing, building, testing, and publishing interactive experiences. It gives students immediate feedback as they apply new concepts.

How can an adult support the experience?

Adults can ask students about their design goals, encourage regular testing, and help them reflect on feedback. The resources are written so families and educators can follow the program.

What safety steps should adults take?

Build Day is a guided learning event. Adults should use their usual family or school settings, review Roblox account and privacy settings, and supervise online participation in accordance with their community’s policies.
