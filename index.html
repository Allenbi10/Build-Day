01 — WHAT IS BUILD DAY?

From player
to creator.

Build Day is a Roblox creator event designed for players who have never made a game before.

Over one weekend, you’ll get a creative challenge, learn the basics of Roblox Studio, build a small experience, and get tips that make your game more fun to play. No coding background or budget required.

R

What is Roblox Studio?

Roblox Studio is the free tool creators use to make Roblox experiences. It lets you shape a world, place objects, design challenges, and test your game before inviting friends to play.
